Race Condition

Understand how overlapping operations on shared data can produce incorrect results when synchronization is missing.

CWE-362 Concurrency Security Lab 03

01. The Vulnerability

A Race Condition happens when the result of operations depends on their timing or interleaving while accessing shared state.

Without appropriate synchronization, multiple requests can read or update the same resource in an unsafe order.
