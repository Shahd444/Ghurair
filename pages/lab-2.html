Race Condition

Explore how concurrent requests and overlapping operations can cause unexpected application behavior and security vulnerabilities.

CWE-362 Concurrency Security Lab 02

01. The Vulnerability

A Race Condition occurs when multiple operations access or modify shared resources concurrently, and the final result depends on the timing or order of their execution.

For example, two requests may check the same account balance before either updates it, potentially allowing both operations to proceed when only one should succeed.

04. Why It Matters

Race Conditions can result in inconsistent data, duplicate transactions, unauthorized actions, and unintended resource modifications.

These vulnerabilities are particularly dangerous in applications that process payments, inventory, account balances, or other shared resources.

05. The Fix

Race Conditions can be prevented by protecting critical operations with appropriate synchronization, atomic transactions, locking mechanisms, or database constraints.
